Privacy Policy · प्राइवेसी पॉलिसी

Chanda Collector by Sakred Studio · Effective 2026-10-23

Short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. Chanda Collector has no server, no account, no analytics, and never moves money.

छोटा जवाब: आप जो भी लिखते हैं वह आपके फ़ोन पर ही रहता है। चंदा कलेक्टर का कोई सर्वर नहीं है, कोई अकाउंट नहीं, कोई ट्रैकिंग नहीं, और यह कभी पैसे ट्रांसफर नहीं करता।

What the app stores

  • Collection names, amounts, deadlines and the people you add (name, phone number, paid / pending status).
  • Your own settings: language, your name and UPI ID (only if you choose to enter them).

All of this is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you uninstall the app or clear site data, it is gone, unless you used Settings → Export Backup to save a JSON file.

What leaves your phone

  • WhatsApp reminders. When you tap a remind button, the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message. Nothing is sent until you press Send inside WhatsApp. The message includes the collection name, the amount, your UPI ID if you set one, and a short "sent with Chanda" line with the app link.
  • UPI QR code. The QR is generated on your phone from the UPI ID you typed. Payments happen in the payer's own UPI app. Chanda Collector never sees, holds or routes money.
  • Backups. Export creates a file on your device. Where you keep or share that file is up to you.

Contacts

If you use "From Contacts", your phone shows its own contact picker and the app receives only the names and numbers you select. The app never reads your whole contact list and never uploads contacts.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts at runtime. The app works fully offline after the first load.

Children

The app is a general-purpose tool for adults organising group collections. It does not knowingly collect data from children.
